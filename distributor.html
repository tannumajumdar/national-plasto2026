    Why Partner with NPPL

    Commercial Advantages for Dealers

    Built on 29 years of manufacturing since 1997, our dealer programme is designed around what grows your business: healthy trade margins, a protected territory to build in, and established brands your customers already trust.

    Attractive Trade Margins

    Competitive dealer pricing that leaves a healthy margin on every piece you sell, with more to earn as your volumes grow.

    • Healthy margins across every price segment
    • Annual target achievement rebates
    • Volume and festival-season trade promotions

    Protected Territories

    Clear regional demarcation ensuring you enjoy exclusive sales distribution rights in your designated district.

    • A defined territory for every appointed partner
    • Room to build your local market without channel conflict
    • Long-term, relationship-led partnerships

    Retail Brand Advantage

    Four established brands — NATIONAL, NEXT, SAPPHIRE and CAPTAIN — give your showroom a ready range from everyday value to premium.

    • 362 models in the 2026 catalogue
    • Showroom display stands and glossy product catalogues
    • Local advertising and point-of-sale collateral support

    Fast, Reliable Supply

    Four ISO-certified plants at Howrah, Guwahati, Roorkee and Hajipur, backed by regional stock depots, keep your shelves filled.

    • 24–48 hour transit across key trade corridors
    • Regional depots for quicker replenishment
    • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality at every plant
