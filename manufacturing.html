    Manufacturing Hubs — Group Locations

    4 integrated plants across India engineered for high throughput, heavy injection tonnage, and tight dimensional precision.

    Industrial Pedigree

    Integrated Manufacturing Plants

    Harnessing computerized injection moulding cells, centralized raw resin blending, and robotic parts removal.

    Main Headquarters • Plant 1

    Dhulagarh Central Facility & Toolroom

    Jalan Industrial Complex, Howrah, West Bengal

    Our flagship manufacturing complex and central tooling workshop. Houses our heaviest 1,200T high-tonnage presses for large monoblock dining tables, executive armchairs, and custom industrial crates.

    1,200 Ton Max Press Tonnage
    24x7 Continuous Automated Shifts
    High-Speed Facility • Plant 2

    Uluberia Monoblock Injection Plant

    Uluberia Industrial Growth Centre, Howrah, WB

    High-speed automated injection moulding cells with multi-cavity moulds, specialized in commercial monoblock seating, stools, and multi-tier modular storage systems.

    18 Presses Operational Fleet
    ISO 9001:2015 Quality Certified
    North-East Hub • Plant 3

    Guwahati Regional Manufacturing Plant

    Amingaon Industrial Estate, Kamrup, Assam

    Strategically positioned manufacturing and regional logistics hub engineered to serve all 7 North-Eastern states with reduced transit times and dedicated regional inventory.

    24-48 Hours Regional Turnaround
    7 States Direct Distribution
    Eastern Hub • Plant 4

    Patna Utility & Storage Complex

    Fatuha Industrial Area, Patna, Bihar

    Modern injection pressing plant engineered for heavy agricultural crates, cold-storage parts, and everyday utility products catering to the dense Central & Northern trade corridors.

    15,000 MT Annual Throughput
    Direct Rail/Road Logistics Siding
    In-House Quality Control

    Precision Tooling & Laboratory Rigor

    Every National Plasto product undergoes rigorous destructive testing before dispatch. Our in-house mould room is equipped with EDM spark erosion, CNC cavity machining, and precision surface polishers to eliminate flash lines and parting-line defects.

    • Melt Flow Index (MFI) Spectrometric Raw Resin Purity Testing
    • 200 kg Static Load Hold Cycles replicate real-world stress conditions
    • 1.5-meter Drop Impact Testing to validate zero polymer embrittlement
    • UV Weather-O-Meter Color-Fastness chamber for outdoor sun endurance
