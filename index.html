    National Plasto Virtues

    National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd., established in 1997, has been delivering precision engineering and premium moulded polymer products for over 29 years across India based on these core tenets.

    Integrity

    Our mission is to operate with integrity, foster a culture of the utmost respect for individuals and serve as a catalyst for development.

    Customer Centricity

    With continuous innovation and efficiency, we aim to provide consistent products and services that improve the quality of life and exceed customer expectations.

    Delivering Excellence

    Our innovations have been backed by unparalleled quality that has withstood the effect of time throughout the years.

    Innovation

    Painstaking efforts and research behind every new development help us serve products that truly benefit humanity and the environment.

    People First

    National Plasto has always placed a great emphasis on taking care of those who are associated with us as they are one of our greatest assets.

    Empowerment

    Our team of experts and our workforce are our biggest assets. Empowering them every step of the way constantly helps us deliver only the best.

    Every business goes through difficult phases. What matters is not avoiding losses, but learning from them and growing bigger and better each time. My father taught me that how you treat people matters as much as how you run a business — that belief has shaped this company since 1997, and it continues to guide how we build for the future.

    Our Divisions

    One Company. Four Distinct Market Propositions.

    From premium virgin-plastic furniture to economical reprocessed-plastic solutions, our four divisions allow us to serve customers across multiple price points and market segments.

    Each division has its own identity, product focus and customer proposition — while collectively contributing to our larger vision of making quality plastic furniture accessible to every space and every need.

    01
    01

    NEXT

    Premium Virgin Plastic Furniture

    NEXT represents our premium segment of plastic moulded furniture, developed for customers seeking a combination of premium materials, contemporary designs, functionality and superior aesthetics.

    The division primarily focuses on products manufactured using virgin plastic, allowing us to develop furniture with a premium look and finish for modern homes and contemporary spaces.

    Explore NEXT range →
    02
    02

    NATIONAL

    Semi-Virgin & Reprocessed Plastic Furniture

    NATIONAL is our established mass-market plastic furniture division, offering a broad range of products designed to provide value, functionality and accessibility to customers across India.

    The division works with a combination of semi-virgin and reprocessed plastic, enabling us to offer a wide product portfolio across different price points.

    NATIONAL is positioned as one of India's leading reprocessed plastic furniture brands, with a strong presence across the market. Its broad product range allows the brand to address diverse requirements across households, businesses, institutions and other establishments.

    Explore NATIONAL range →

    CAPTAIN

    Economical Reprocessed Plastic Furniture

    CAPTAIN is focused on providing economical plastic furniture solutions using reprocessed plastic.

    The division is designed around affordability, practicality and high-volume everyday use, making it particularly relevant to customers in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities and towns.

    Explore CAPTAIN range →
    04

    SAPPHIRE

    Economical Reprocessed Plastic Furniture

    SAPPHIRE operates in a similar economical segment to CAPTAIN, with a distinct focus within the chair, table, stool and baby chair category.

    The division uses reprocessed plastic and is positioned to address the requirements of price-conscious markets, particularly across Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

    While CAPTAIN has a broader focus covering chairs, tables and children's furniture, SAPPHIRE provides a separate and differentiated range of chairs, allowing us to address another segment of the market.

    Sustainability

    Know More

    At National Plasto, responsible manufacturing is part of how we make furniture for every space and every need. Our NATIONAL, CAPTAIN and SAPPHIRE ranges are built with reprocessed plastic, giving used polymer a long second life as durable everyday furniture.

    Every one of our four plants — Howrah, Guwahati, Roorkee and Hajipur — is independently certified for environmental management, and our energy use is managed under ISO 50001.

    We believe every manufacturer has a responsibility to make choices that benefit the environment and the people who work with us. That is why we continue to invest in safer workplaces, efficient processes and products that last for years.
